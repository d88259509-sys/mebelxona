The Workshop Way

From Raw Timber to Your Living Room

Every piece undergoes 5 rigorous artisanal stages to ensure lifelong endurance.

01

3D Consultation

We measure your space, discuss functional needs, and build a photorealistic 3D model with material samples.

02

Timber Selection

Selecting properly dried solid beech, oak, or walnut with unique grain character and moisture grade <8%.

03

Precision Joinery

Mortise-and-tenon joints, dovetail drawer corners, and whisper-quiet Austrian concealed hardware.

04

Organic Finishing

Triple-layer hardwax oil and natural beeswax hand-buffed to preserve tactile warmth and liquid resistance.

05

White-Glove Delivery

Assembled in your room by our master carpenters, leveled precisely, cleaned, and signed off under warranty.
